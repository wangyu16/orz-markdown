## London dispersion

Present between all molecules; grows with polarizability (molar mass and
surface area).

## Dipole–dipole

Between polar molecules; aligns partial charges $\delta^+$ and $\delta^-$.

## Hydrogen bonding

A strong dipole–dipole interaction when H is bonded to N, O or F:
$\ce{H2O}$ boils at 100 °C while $\ce{H2S}$ boils at −60 °C.
