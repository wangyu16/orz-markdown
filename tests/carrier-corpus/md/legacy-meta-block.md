# Introduction to Polymers

This file predates format 1. Its metadata lives in a leading `kind: meta`
block (DocMeta, FC-03), which a format-1 reader takes as the header when no
`kind: document` block leads the text.

## Chain growth

Monomers add one at a time to an active chain end: radical, cationic, or
anionic. Molecular weight is high from the start.

## Step growth

Any two species can react; high molecular weight appears only at very high
conversion (Carothers: $\bar{X}_n = \frac{1}{1-p}$).
